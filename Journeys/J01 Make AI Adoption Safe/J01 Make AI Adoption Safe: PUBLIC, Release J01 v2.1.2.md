J01 Make AI Adoption Safe: PUBLIC, Release J01 v2.1

J01 sets out to make the enormous problem of AI adoption small enough to work through and test.

This release updates the free PUBLIC pack. It is the first to draw on what we learned from a facilitated V1 workshop pilot of J01 at the South Coast User Group on 22 October 2026. The pilot gave encouraging signals. It also showed us that the experience was too complex: too much material, too many terms and models, inconsistent language, too many steps, and not enough time to make sense of the harder activities afterwards. At times it overloaded participants and facilitators alike. We are treating this as learning to act on, not as proof that the approach works.

In this release:

a new Start Here with a simple route through the pack;
updated Public Journey Guide, Summary, Journey Deck and Risk Spine model (now with alt text);
Module Cards, Knowledge Editions and References aligned with the current module masters;
a new card: How to tell whether oversight is working;
updated L0 Journey Check and Where to Go Next.
The six-stage Risk Spine is unchanged. Release 2 (27 October 2026) remains available in this repository's history.
